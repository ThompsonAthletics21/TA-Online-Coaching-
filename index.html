Functional Training

This program is built for people who want to move better in everyday life. The focus is on building core stability, strengthening the tendons and joints so they can handle load through a full range of motion, and developing the mobility, balance, and body control that most training programs ignore entirely. Progression here is driven by how well and how easily you move — not just by the numbers on the bar.
What's included
  • Fully custom functional program — built around your current movement quality, limitations, and goals
  • Core stability training — targeted work to build the foundation that supports every movement you make
  • Joint & tendon resilience — progressive loading through range of motion to strengthen connective tissue over time
  • Weekly check-ins — movement footage review, exercise execution coaching and cueing, and program adjustments
  • Progression based on movement quality — exercises advance as your form and ease of movement improve, not just when numbers go up
  • Full DM access — throughout the month for questions and feedback
  • Delivered via app — your program lives in one place, easy to follow
What this program targets
  • 1
    Mobility Improving the usable range of motion in your joints so your body can move freely without restriction or compensation.
  • 2
    Stability & balance Building the neuromuscular control to hold positions and move through them with intention — not just strength.
  • 3
    Core strength Deep, functional core work that transfers directly to how you feel and move — in and out of the gym.
  • 4
    Joint & tendon health Gradual, intentional loading through range of motion to build connective tissue that can handle stress long-term.
